# Support

## Installing and using it

The [companion app page](https://dynamicassist.err403.com/docs/companion-app/) on the Dynamic Assist website covers installing it, the permission sets users need, and what happens when it's missing. The [README](README.md) has the same, plus its REST endpoints. The [Dynamic Assist](https://github.com/garethcheyne/Dynamic-Assist) repository covers the browser extension that uses it.

## Problems and ideas

Use the [feedback page](https://dynamicassist.err403.com/feedback/?product=companion), or [open an issue](https://github.com/garethcheyne/Dynamic-Assist_BusinessCentralAddon/issues) here. Include:

- the companion version, from Extension Management
- the Business Central version, from **Help & Support**
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened, including any error message

For a security problem, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

This is an independent project, maintained in spare time. It isn't supported by Microsoft.
